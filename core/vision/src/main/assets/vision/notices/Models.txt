Adaptations: shared components, explicit failures, pixel coordinates, cancellation,
tiling, complete-region deduplication, strict CTC dictionaries and vertical glyph candidates.

OCR weights and dictionaries: PaddlePaddle official Hugging Face repositories,
Apache-2.0 according to the respective model cards.
https://huggingface.co/PaddlePaddle/PP-OCRv6_small_det_onnx
https://huggingface.co/PaddlePaddle/PP-OCRv6_small_rec_onnx
https://huggingface.co/PaddlePaddle/korean_PP-OCRv5_mobile_rec_onnx
Dictionaries derived without reordering from the matching inference.yml.

Seg weights: mixed-dual-s-e5_float16.tflite extracted from the author's v3.5.5 APK.
https://github.com/jedzqer/manga-translator-android/releases/tag/v3.5.5

identified in the source checkout or distribution. The code's MIT license is not
asserted to license these weights. Current model preparation is for self-use/testing;
